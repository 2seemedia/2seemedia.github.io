Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our services

What services does 2SEE Media offer?

We offer visual production, photography, video filming and editing, content creation, social media management, graphic design and digital advertising campaigns.

How can I request a quote?

Complete the quote form with your services, goals, content quantities and estimated budget, then send it via WhatsApp or Gmail. We review the details and discuss the scope before preparing a quote.

Do you offer monthly social media packages?

Yes. Our Basic and Premium packages combine content management and production at different levels. See the packages section for quantities, prices and included services.

Can I book filming and editing without account management?

Yes. Photography, filming and editing can be booked separately. The quote defines the scope, content quantities and deliverables.

Do packages include ideas and scripts?

The listed content management packages include ideas, scripts and a monthly content plan. For standalone filming or editing, scriptwriting is included only when agreed in the scope.

Do you have a photography studio?

Yes. 2SEE Media has a studio in Damascus. We confirm its suitability and required setup for each project, and can discuss filming at another location.

How long does production and delivery take?

Timing depends on the scope, content quantities and production requirements. We agree shoot dates, review stages and delivery deadlines before starting.

Can videos or designs be revised after delivery?

Yes, within the revision rounds specified in your package or agreement. Reshoots and changes outside the agreed scope are discussed and quoted separately.

Does social media management include replies to messages and comments?

This depends on the package and agreed scope. We confirm who handles replies, which platforms are covered and the response hours before starting. Around-the-clock replies are not included unless explicitly agreed.

Do your prices include advertising spend?

No. Advertising spend is separate from service and campaign management fees. We specify both the media budget and management fees in the quote before launching a campaign.

Can you work with companies outside Syria?

We can discuss remote projects such as editing, design, content creation and digital management. Feasibility, file delivery and communication are confirmed for each project. Filming outside Syria requires a separate arrangement.

How can I join the 2SEE team?

Visit Join our team, select your specialty, prepare your CV and work links, then send your application via WhatsApp or Gmail. We welcome future collaboration applications; listed specialties do not indicate current vacancies.

Didn’t find the answer you were looking for?

Our team is ready to discuss your idea and help you choose the right service.
